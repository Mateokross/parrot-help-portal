Voice cloning replicates an author's unique voice — their tone, cadence, and character — and uses it to narrate that author's articles in AI-generated audio. Instead of a generic AI voice reading every piece, readers hear the familiar sound of the writer they follow. Publishers who have activated voice cloning see **2–3× increased usage** on articles narrated by the author's own voice, and Clarín reported a 10× increase in audio usage after adding voice cloning to their player.

## How it works

Once a voice model is created for an author, every article attributed to them is automatically narrated in their cloned voice — no manual steps required after setup.

1. **A voice model is trained** from a recording of the author's voice. Powerbeans handles the technical process of creating a high-fidelity voice model that captures the author's natural speech patterns.
2. **The model is assigned to the author** in your Powerbeans dashboard. From that point forward, all of their articles are narrated using the cloned voice automatically.
3. **Readers recognize the voice**, which builds trust, creates a stronger sense of connection with the writer, and drives longer listening sessions.

## Prerequisites

Before any voice cloning work begins, you must satisfy the following requirements. These are non-negotiable and enforced by Powerbeans before any voice model is activated.

- **Explicit written consent is mandatory.** The author whose voice is being cloned must give clear, informed, written consent prior to any recording or model training. Verbal consent is not sufficient.
- **A Voice Cloning Consent Form must be completed.** This is a formal document required by Powerbeans. Request the form from [legal@powerbeans.ai](mailto:legal@powerbeans.ai) before scheduling any recordings.
- **Powerbeans may require proof of consent.** Before activating a custom voice model, Powerbeans may ask you to provide the signed consent form as evidence that the author has agreed to the process.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm written consent">
    Before anything else, confirm that the author has given explicit written consent for their voice to be cloned. Do not proceed without this — consent must be secured before any recording takes place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request the Consent Form">
    Email [legal@powerbeans.ai](mailto:legal@powerbeans.ai) to request the Voice Cloning Consent Form for your author. Powerbeans will send you the current version of the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Author completes the form and records a sample">
    Have the author read and sign the consent form. At the same time, they'll need to record a voice sample — Powerbeans provides clear recording instructions, including what to say, what equipment to use, and how to ensure a clean recording.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the form and recording to Powerbeans">
    Send the completed consent form and voice recording to Powerbeans. Your account manager will confirm receipt and move the submission into the training queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Powerbeans trains the voice model">
    Powerbeans processes the recording and trains a custom voice model. Turnaround time depends on recording quality and current queue volume — your account manager will confirm an estimated timeline once the consent form and recording are submitted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cloned voice is activated in your dashboard">
    Once training is complete, the cloned voice is activated in your Powerbeans dashboard and automatically assigned to the author's articles. You'll receive a confirmation from your account manager when it's live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules and restrictions

<Warning>
  Voice cloning is subject to strict usage policies. Violations may result in immediate deactivation of the cloned voice and suspension of your account.

  - **You must not clone a voice without the speaker's explicit written consent.** This applies to all individuals, including public figures, journalists, and staff.
  - **You must not use voice cloning to impersonate or mislead listeners.** Cloned voices may only be used to narrate content genuinely authored by the person whose voice was cloned.
  - **Powerbeans will deactivate any cloned voice if consent is withdrawn.** If an author revokes their consent at any time, notify Powerbeans immediately at [legal@powerbeans.ai](mailto:legal@powerbeans.ai). The voice will be deactivated and their articles will revert to a standard AI voice.
  - **Misuse will result in deactivation.** Any use of voice cloning outside its intended purpose — including use in ads, third-party content, or synthetic media not authored by the cloned individual — is a breach of the Powerbeans usage policy.
</Warning>
