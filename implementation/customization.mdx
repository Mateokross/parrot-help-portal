Every Powerbeans widget is fully customizable from the [app.powerbeans.ai](https://app.powerbeans.ai) dashboard — no code changes required after the initial embed. The goal is to make widgets feel like a native part of your site, so your readers get a seamless experience that matches your publication's visual identity and editorial voice.

<Note>
  Advanced customization options — including full color and font control and layout flexibility — are available from the **Pro plan** and above. Basic customization (player texts and default voice selection) is available on all plans.
</Note>

## Pronunciation Dictionary

Available from the **Pro plan**, the Pronunciation Dictionary lets you correct the AI voice's pronunciation for words specific to your publication — things like brand names, product names, foreign place names, or industry acronyms that standard text-to-speech tends to mispronounce.

You manage pronunciation entries from the dashboard under **Settings → Pronunciation**. Each entry maps a written word or phrase to the correct spoken form, using either a phonetic spelling or an alternate word that sounds right when read aloud.

Common use cases include:
- **Brand and proper names**: ensure your publication's name, partner brands, and prominent figures are pronounced correctly
- **Acronyms**: control whether an acronym is spoken letter-by-letter ("A-I") or as a word ("ay")
- **Regional place names**: correct pronunciation for cities, regions, or landmarks that differ from standard dictionary entries

## Voice Selection

The number of voices available to you depends on your plan:

| Plan | Voices included |
|------|----------------|
| Starter | 1 voice |
| Pro | 2 voices |
| Scale | 4 voices |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

Voices can be assigned globally or configured per language and per publication section — for example, using one voice for news and another for opinion pieces, or matching voices to the language of each article. Enterprise publishers get access to exclusive, premium voices not available on other plans.

## Carousel

Available from the **Pro plan**, Carousel enables continuous, hands-free audio playback by automatically queuing and playing the next article when the current one ends. This keeps listeners engaged without requiring them to tap or click — ideal for commuters, multitaskers, and audio-first readers.

Publishers who enable Carousel see **1.5× to 2.5× improvement in audio completion rates** compared to standard single-article playback. You can enable or disable Carousel from the **Audio Player** settings in your dashboard.

<Tip>
  Work with your Powerbeans account manager during onboarding to configure the best widget setup for your site's look and feel. They can help you select voices, dial in colors, and set up Carousel to maximize engagement from day one.
</Tip>
