Each Powerbeans widget embeds with a single self-contained `<script>` tag — no build tools, no npm packages, no framework dependencies. When a reader loads your page, the script injects the widget into the DOM and authenticates with the Powerbeans platform using your API key. From there, Powerbeans handles content ingestion, audio generation, and delivery automatically.

<Note>
  Replace `YOUR_POWERBEANS_TOKEN` with your actual API key. You'll find it inside the implementation guide for the relevant widget in your [app.powerbeans.ai](https://app.powerbeans.ai) dashboard.
</Note>

## Widget embed codes

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Audio Player">
    The Audio Player widget adds AI-narrated audio directly to your article pages. Paste the snippet inside the `<body>` of your CMS template, just before the closing `</body>` tag, so it appears on every article page.

    ```html
    <script id="pb-scr">!function(){var t=document.createElement("script");t.src="https://dev.powerbeans-ai.com/powerbeans-widget-latest.js",document.body.appendChild(t);var r=document.createElement("div");r.id="powerbeans-widget",r.setAttribute("apiKey","YOUR_POWERBEANS_TOKEN");var a=document.getElementById('pb-scr');a.parentNode.insertBefore(r,a.nextSibling)}();</script>
    ```

    Once the script is live, Powerbeans automatically crawls the article content on each page and generates audio — no editorial work required.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Playlist">
    The Playlist widget turns your article catalog into a bingeable audio playlist. You can embed it on your homepage, in a dedicated section, or as a floating button — wherever your readers are most likely to discover it.

    ```html
    <script id="pb-scr">!function(){var t=document.createElement("script");t.src="https://dev.powerbeans-ai.com/powerbeans-playlist-latest.umd.js",document.body.appendChild(t);var q=document.createElement("link");q.rel="stylesheet"; q.href="https://dev.powerbeans-ai.com/powerbeans-playlist-latest.css",document.body.appendChild(q);var r=document.createElement("div");r.id="powerbeans-playlist",r.setAttribute("data-api-key","YOUR_POWERBEANS_TOKEN"),r.setAttribute("data-playlists","YOUR_PLAYLISTS_OR_LEAVE_EMPTY");var a=document.getElementById('pb-scr');a.parentNode.insertBefore(r,a.nextSibling)}();</script>
    ```

    The `data-playlists` attribute accepts a comma-separated list of playlist IDs from your dashboard. Leave the value empty and Powerbeans will select and sequence articles automatically based on your catalog.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Text Summaries">
    The Text Summaries widget displays an AI-generated overview at the top of each article, helping readers decide what to read next. Paste the snippet on each article page where you want the summary to appear.

    ```html
    <script id="pb-scr">!function(){var t=document.createElement("script");t.src="https://dev.powerbeans-ai.com/powerbeans-widget-latest.js",document.body.appendChild(t);var r=document.createElement("div");r.id="powerbeans-summary",r.setAttribute("apiKey","YOUR_POWERBEANS_TOKEN");var a=document.getElementById('pb-scr');a.parentNode.insertBefore(r,a.nextSibling)}();</script>
    ```

    81% of readers find summaries useful, and users who see them explore 2× more pages per session.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Performance

All Powerbeans widgets are engineered to have zero impact on your Core Web Vitals. Each widget script loads **asynchronously** — meaning it runs in the background, completely independently of your page's main thread and critical rendering path. The widget assets are served from a global CDN and **cached in the browser for 30 days**, so returning readers experience near-instant load times without hitting Powerbeans' servers on every visit.

## Accessibility

The Audio Player and Playlist widgets pass **WCAG 2.2** by default — proper ARIA labels, visible focus states for keyboard navigation, and screen-reader-friendly playback text throughout, with no additional work required after embedding. The Text Summaries widget's accessibility card is not yet live (fixes are pending) — see the [Accessibility overview](/accessibility) for the full breakdown by product.

## Troubleshooting

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Widget not appearing on the page" icon="eye-slash">
    Verify that `YOUR_POWERBEANS_TOKEN` has been replaced with your actual API key from the dashboard. Then open your browser's developer console (F12) and check for any JavaScript errors or network failures that may indicate a misconfiguration.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Audio is not generating" icon="waveform">
    Allow a few minutes after embedding — Powerbeans crawls your article content automatically after the widget is first detected on a page. If audio still hasn't appeared after 15–30 minutes, confirm that your article pages are publicly accessible (not behind a login or paywall) and reach out to [support@powerbeans.ai](mailto:support@powerbeans.ai).
  </Card>

  </Card>
</CardGroup>
