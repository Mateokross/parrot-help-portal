The Powerbeans dashboard at [app.powerbeans.ai](https://app.powerbeans.ai) gives you full visibility into how your products are performing — engagement, audio, and revenue all in one place. Whether you're tracking time on site, monitoring audio completion rates, or reviewing ad revenue, every key metric is available without leaving the platform.

## Accessing the Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in at app.powerbeans.ai">
    Go to [app.powerbeans.ai](https://app.powerbeans.ai) and log in with your publisher credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your site">
    Use the **site selector** at the top of the screen to choose the domain you want to review. Each site has its own independent data view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Analytics">
    Click **Analytics** in the left-hand menu to open the full reporting view. Your metrics load for the default date range automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's in the Dashboard

The Analytics section is organized into three reporting areas, each focused on a different aspect of your Powerbeans deployment.

### Engagement Metrics

These metrics show how readers are interacting with your content on pages where Powerbeans products are active.

- **Time on site** — Average time readers spend on pages where at least one Powerbeans widget is embedded. An increase here is a direct signal of stronger content engagement driven by audio and summaries.
- **Bounce rate** — Percentage of visitors who leave after viewing only one page. A lower bounce rate means readers are sticking around and exploring more of your content.
- **Articles read per session** — Average number of articles a reader views in a single visit. The Carousel and Playlist features are the primary drivers of this metric.
- **Audio completion rate** — Percentage of audio plays that were listened to completion. Powerbeans publishers average 85% completion across the network.

### Audio Performance

These metrics track how your audio content specifically is being consumed.

- **Plays started** — Total number of times a user pressed play on any audio widget (Audio Player or Playlist).
- **Completions** — Number of audio plays that ran through to the end.
- **Average listen time** — Mean duration of audio sessions, including partial listens.
- **Carousel usage** — Number of sessions in which the Carousel feature auto-played at least one additional article after the first.

### Monetization Metrics

These metrics reflect advertising activity and revenue generated through the Powerbeans ad network.

- **Ad impressions** — Number of times an ad was displayed or played within your Powerbeans widgets.
- **Fill rate** — Percentage of ad requests that were matched with a paying ad.
- **CPM** — Revenue earned per 1,000 ad impressions, reflecting the quality of demand filling your inventory.
- **Total revenue** — Your estimated share of ad revenue for the selected period.
- **Estimated vs. reconciled amounts** — The dashboard shows an estimate in real time; the final confirmed amount is available after monthly reconciliation.

## Date Range and Filtering

Use the **date range picker** at the top of the Analytics page to focus on a specific period — daily, weekly, monthly, or a custom range. You can also filter metrics by product to see data for the **Audio Player**, **Playlist**, or **Summaries** independently. This is useful for comparing performance across products or isolating the impact of a recent configuration change.

<Note>
  Monetization figures shown in the dashboard are estimates based on real-time reporting from demand partners. Final reconciled amounts are confirmed after monthly reconciliation and may differ slightly from the in-dashboard totals.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If you have multiple sites under the same Powerbeans account, use the site selector to view metrics for each domain separately. Aggregate views across all sites are not combined automatically — switch between sites to compare performance.
</Tip>

For a full explanation of what each metric measures and how it is calculated, see the [Metrics reference](/reporting/metrics).
