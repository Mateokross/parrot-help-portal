This page defines every metric shown in your Powerbeans dashboard, so you understand exactly what's being measured and how. Use it as a reference alongside the live data in [Analytics at app.powerbeans.ai](https://app.powerbeans.ai) to interpret trends and make informed decisions about your content and configuration.

## Engagement Metrics

Engagement metrics measure how readers interact with your content on pages where Powerbeans products are active. These are the clearest signals of whether your audio and summary widgets are driving deeper session behaviour.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Time on Site">
    **What it measures:** The average time readers spend on pages where at least one Powerbeans product is active.

    **Why it matters:** An increase in time on site shows that your audience is engaging more deeply with your content rather than skimming and leaving. Publishers on the Powerbeans network see an average increase of +155% in time on site after going live. Audio and summaries give readers more to consume, extending each visit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Bounce Rate">
    **What it measures:** The percentage of visitors who leave your site after viewing only one page without any further interaction.

    **Why it matters:** A lower bounce rate means readers are clicking through to more of your content. Powerbeans reduces bounce rate by giving readers additional ways to engage — including the Carousel's auto-play feature and Playlist embeds that keep attention on your site. Publishers typically see a reduction of around 33% in bounce rate after activation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Articles Read per Session">
    **What it measures:** The average number of article pages a reader views within a single session.

    **Why it matters:** This metric captures how effectively your content draws readers from one article to the next. Powerbeans' Carousel feature — which automatically queues the next article's audio when one ends — and the Playlist widget are the primary drivers. Publishers see an average of +45% more articles read per session.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="CTR (Click-Through Rate)">
    **What it measures:** The percentage of users who click on a linked interactive element — most commonly the next-article link in the Carousel or a Playlist item.

    **Why it matters:** CTR reflects how compelling your content is to readers already engaged with a Powerbeans widget. Powerbeans benchmarks a greater-than-50% CTR improvement versus competitor players, meaning readers are more likely to follow through to related content.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Audio Performance Metrics

Audio performance metrics track how readers consume your AI-narrated audio content, across both the Audio Player and Playlist products.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Plays Started">
    **What it measures:** The total number of times a user pressed play on any audio widget — including the Audio Player on individual articles and the standalone Playlist.

    **Why it matters:** Plays started is the top-of-funnel audio metric. A high plays-started count indicates that your widgets are visible and positioned well, and that readers are choosing to listen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Audio Completion Rate">
    **What it measures:** The percentage of audio plays that ran through to the end of the content.

    **Why it matters:** A high completion rate shows that your audio content is holding listeners' attention. The Powerbeans network average is **85% completion** — significantly above industry norms for on-site audio. This is driven by short-form summary audio, voice quality, and the Carousel's seamless article-to-article transitions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Average Listen Time">
    **What it measures:** The mean duration of audio sessions across all plays, including partial listens.

    **Why it matters:** Average listen time helps you understand whether readers are dipping in briefly or committing to longer sessions. Combined with completion rate, it gives a fuller picture of audio engagement quality.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Carousel Sessions">
    **What it measures:** The number of sessions in which the Carousel feature auto-played at least one additional article after the first audio completed.

    **Why it matters:** Carousel sessions measure how effectively the auto-play experience is driving readers from one article to the next without requiring manual interaction. A growing Carousel session count correlates directly with increases in articles read per session and time on site.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Monetization Metrics

Monetization metrics track advertising activity and revenue generated through the Powerbeans ad network across all products. These are available to publishers with monetization enabled on their plan.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Ad Impressions">
    **What it measures:** The number of times an ad was successfully displayed or played within a Powerbeans widget — including display banners, audio pre-roll/post-roll, and video formats.

    **Why it matters:** Impressions are the raw volume metric for your ad inventory. More impressions mean more monetization opportunities. The Powerbeans Carousel can deliver up to 6× more ad opportunities per session compared with standard audio players by creating new ad breaks between articles.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Fill Rate">
    **What it measures:** The percentage of ad requests sent to demand partners that were returned with a paying ad.

    **Why it matters:** A higher fill rate means more of your available inventory is generating revenue. Powerbeans is connected to Google AdX (via MCM), 35+ SSPs, and private marketplace deals, which increases competitive demand and keeps fill rates high.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="CPM (Cost Per Mille)">
    **What it measures:** The revenue earned per 1,000 ad impressions delivered within your Powerbeans widgets.

    **Why it matters:** CPM reflects the quality of demand filling your inventory. Audio and video ad formats typically command higher CPMs than standard display. Powerbeans manages demand optimisation end to end, including header bidding across all connected partners.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Estimated Revenue">
    **What it measures:** Your share of advertising revenue earned in the selected period, calculated in real time based on reporting received from demand partners.

    **Why it matters:** This gives you a timely view of monetization performance. Note that this figure is an estimate — demand partners may adjust reported numbers during the monthly reconciliation process, so the final amount may differ slightly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reconciled Revenue">
    **What it measures:** The confirmed final revenue amount after adjustments from demand partners have been applied and the monthly reconciliation process is complete.

    **Why it matters:** Reconciled revenue is the authoritative figure used for payouts. It is processed monthly, and payouts are made within 60 calendar days of the end of the month in which revenues were received from demand partners. A minimum payout threshold of USD 200 applies.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  All metrics are calculated based on events fired by the Powerbeans widgets as readers interact with audio, playlists, and summaries. Events use pseudonymous identifiers — no personally identifying information about your readers is collected or stored. For more on data handling and consent, see [GDPR & Consent](/compliance/gdpr-consent).
</Note>
