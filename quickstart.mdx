Get from zero to live AI audio on your articles in three steps — no editorial work required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Sign up at [dashboard.powerbeans.ai](https://dashboard.powerbeans.ai). You'll register your site and see an instant demo — the Player running on one of your own articles — before you touch any code.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Embed the Widget">
    Add one script tag to your article template. Add it once, and every article on that template gets the Player automatically — no per-article setup.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go Live">
    Once the widget is live, Powerbeans ingests your published articles and generates AI audio automatically. Your dashboard shows ingestion status, audio performance, and monetization metrics as content starts flowing in.
  </Step>
</Steps>
