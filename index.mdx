## How It Works

Getting from zero to live audio takes three steps. Powerbeans handles the heavy lifting at every stage.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Embed the Widget">
    Paste one snippet of JavaScript into your CMS template. It loads asynchronously so it never slows your page down.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Content Is Ingested">
    Powerbeans connects to your existing content feed or CMS via integration. New articles are picked up automatically — no manual uploads required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Audio Goes Live">
    High-quality AI audio, playlists, and summaries are generated and published directly on your articles. Ads run automatically through Google MCM.
  </Step>
</Steps>
