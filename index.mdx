Welcome to Powerbeans — AI-powered engagement products built for publishers. With a single line of code embedded in your CMS, Powerbeans automatically converts your existing articles into AI-narrated audio, bingeable playlists, and text summaries, while layering in monetization through built-in ad formats. No editorial work, no new hires, no new tools.
