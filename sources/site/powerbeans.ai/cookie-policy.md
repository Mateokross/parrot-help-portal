# Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 13th, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Powerbeans LTD (“Powerbeans”, “we”, “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies (pixels, local storage, SDKs — together, “cookies”) on our website at powerbeans.ai (the “Website”) and our publisher platform at app.powerbeans.ai (the “Platform”). It should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](https://powerbeans.ai/privacy-policy). For how cookies operate when our audio players, playlists and summaries run on a publisher’s website, see section 5 — in that context the publisher is the controller and the publisher’s own cookie policy applies.

## 1\. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. They allow the site to recognise your device, keep you signed in, remember preferences, and measure how the site is used. Cookies set by us are “first-party”; cookies set by other companies through our pages are “third-party”. Cookies may be “session” cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or “persistent” cookies (retained for a set period).

## 2\. How we use cookies

We use the following categories on the Website and Platform:

Strictly necessary. Required for the Website and Platform to function: authentication and session management, security and bot protection, load balancing, and remembering your cookie choices. These cannot be switched off through our banner because the services cannot operate without them.

Analytics.Help us understand how visitors use the Website and Platform — pages visited, time on page, errors — so we can improve them. We use Google Analytics for this purpose. Set only with your consent where required by law.

Functional. Remember choices such as language and interface preferences to personalise your experience. Set only with your consent where required by law.

## 3\. Cookies we use

The cookies and similar technologies we use are a mix of first-party (set by Powerbeans) and third-party. On the Website, the only third-party cookies are set by Google Analytics, and only after you consent. On the Platform, sign-in and payment cookies are strictly necessary: we use Supabase for authentication and Stripe for payment security. Some are session cookies, deleted when you close your browser; others are persistent cookies retained for a limited period, and your cookie choices are stored for up to 12 months. You can inspect the exact cookies set at any time through your browser’s developer tools, or contact [privacy@powerbeans.ai](mailto:privacy@powerbeans.ai) for more detail.

## 4\. Your choices

Where required by law, non-essential cookies are set only after you consent through our cookie banner, and you can change or withdraw your choices at any time via the “Cookie settings” link in the Website footer. You can also control cookies through your browser settings, including deleting existing cookies and blocking new ones; blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the Website or Platform from working. For Google Analytics specifically, Google provides an opt-out browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

## 5\. Cookies on publisher websites

When you interact with Powerbeans-powered audio players, playlists or summaries embedded on a publisher’s website, cookies and similar identifiers may be used to deliver playback, measure engagement and — where the publisher has enabled monetisation — support advertising. In that context:

The publisher is the controller, and its cookie policy and consent banner govern; Powerbeans processes this data on the publisher’s behalf under our [Data Processing Agreement](https://powerbeans.ai/dpa).

The Services are consent-aware: consent-dependent identifiers and personalised advertising features operate only where the publisher’s consent management platform passes a valid consent signal (including IAB TCF signals where applicable). Absent consent, the Services operate in a limited mode without consent-dependent identifiers.

By design, these identifiers are pseudonymous — we do not collect names, email addresses or other directly identifying information about listeners through the Services.

To exercise privacy rights in relation to a publisher’s website, contact the publisher or use the controls on its site.

## 6\. Changes and contact

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time, including to reflect changes to the cookies we use; the “Last updated” date shows the current version. Questions: [privacy@powerbeans.ai](mailto:privacy@powerbeans.ai).
