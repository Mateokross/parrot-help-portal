# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13th July, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Powerbeans LTD, a company registered in England and Wales under company number 15423977, with registered office at 5 Brayford Square, London, United Kingdom, E1 0SG (“Powerbeans”, “we”, “us”), collects, uses and shares personal data, and the rights you have. It applies to our website at powerbeans.ai (the “Website”), our publisher platform at app.powerbeans.ai (the “Platform”), and our AI audio, summaries, playlists, analytics and monetisation services for publishers (the “Services”).

Privacy contact: [privacy@powerbeans.ai](mailto:privacy@powerbeans.ai), or by post to the registered office above. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or your local supervisory authority; we would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

## 1\. Our two roles: controller and processor

It matters how you interact with us, because our role — and which document governs — differs.

We act as controller when we process personal data for our own purposes: operating the Website, managing publisher accounts and billing, providing support, marketing, preventing fraud and abuse, and complying with law. This Policy describes that processing.

We act as processor when we process data on behalf of our publisher customers — for example, when you listen to audio or read a summary on a publisher’s website that uses the Services. In that case the publisher is the controller, the processing is governed by our [Data Processing Agreement](https://powerbeans.ai/dpa) with the publisher, and this Policy does not describe the publisher’s practices. To understand how a publisher uses your data, please read the privacy policy of the website where you encountered the content.

If you are an employee, contractor or job applicant of Powerbeans, separate privacy notices apply.

## 2\. What we collect

2.1 Publisher account holders and their teams

When you create or use a Platform account we collect: name, business email address, password credentials, company name and details, language preference, team invitations, site domains and configuration, API keys and integration status, support communications, and records of your plan, transactions, invoices and payment events. Payments are processed by our payment processor (currently Stripe); we do not store full payment card numbers on our systems.

2.2 Website visitors and business contacts

When you visit the Website, contact us, book a demo or subscribe to communications, we collect: information you submit in forms (such as name, business email, company, message), and technical data collected automatically — IP address, general location derived from IP, device and browser type, pages viewed and interactions — through cookies and similar technologies (see section 7).

2.3 End users of publisher properties

When the Services run on a publisher’s website, we process on the publisher’s behalf: playback and engagement events, advertising events, consent-dependent identifiers (such as cookie IDs), device and browser information, and general location derived from IP address. By design, we do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or other directly identifying information about end users through the Services. This data is processed under our Data Processing Agreement with the relevant publisher, who acts as controller.

2.4 Voice materials

Where a publisher uses custom or cloned voices, we process the voice recordings, names and consent documentation provided by the publisher for that purpose, on the publisher’s behalf and subject to a mandatory signed Voice Cloning Consent Form.

We may aggregate or de-identify data so that it no longer identifies anyone; such data is not personal data and we may use it for analytics, benchmarking and product improvement.

## 3\. How and why we use personal data

Where we act as controller, we rely on the following legal bases under UK GDPR and, where applicable, EU GDPR:

| Purpose | Examples | Legal basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Providing the Platform and Services | Account creation, authentication, configuration, generation, dashboards, support | Performance of a contract |
| Billing and payments | Charging subscription fees, invoicing, payout administration, debt recovery | Performance of a contract; legal obligation |
| Security and fraud prevention | Detecting abuse, invalid traffic, unauthorised access; protecting the Services | Legitimate interests |
| Product improvement and analytics | Understanding usage of the Website and Platform, improving reliability and features | Legitimate interests; consent where required for cookies |
| Marketing and communications | Newsletters, product updates, demo follow-ups, event invitations | Legitimate interests for business contacts; consent where required; you can opt out at any time |
| Legal compliance | Tax and accounting records, responding to lawful requests, enforcing our terms | Legal obligation; legitimate interests |

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have balanced those interests against your rights and freedoms. You may object at any time (section 8).

## 4\. Who we share personal data with

We share personal data with: (a) service providers who support our business — cloud hosting and infrastructure (Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud), authentication and account infrastructure (Supabase), content delivery and security (Cloudflare), AI generation providers (OpenAI, Anthropic), analytics (Google Analytics), payment processing (Stripe), and business email and collaboration (Google Workspace); (b) advertising demand and measurement partners, where monetisation is enabled and subject to applicable consent signals; (c) professional advisers such as auditors, accountants and lawyers; (d) regulators, law enforcement or courts where required by law or to protect our rights, users or the Services; and (e) a purchaser or successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal data.

## 5\. International transfers

Personal data may be processed in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area and other countries where our service providers operate. Where personal data is transferred outside the UK or EEA to a country without an adequacy decision, we put appropriate safeguards in place, including (as applicable) the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified providers, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, and/or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, together with supplementary measures where required.

## 6\. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy:

Account, billing and transactional records: six (6) years, for tax, accounting and legal compliance.

Publisher content and AI outputs (including any personal data within them): deleted or anonymised within ninety (90) days of termination of the publisher’s agreement.

Technical logs: retained for up to six (6) months.

Marketing data: until you opt out or we no longer have a lawful basis.

We may retain data longer where required by law or necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it securely.

## 7\. Cookies and similar technologies

On the Website and Platform we use: (a) essential cookies, necessary for sign-in, security and core functionality; (b) analytics cookies, to understand how the Website and Platform are used and improve them; and (c) where applicable, advertising and measurement cookies. Non-essential cookies are used only with your consent where required by law, and you can manage preferences through our cookie banner or your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect functionality. See our [Cookie Policy](https://powerbeans.ai/cookie-policy) for details.

On publisher websites, the setting and reading of cookies and identifiers by the Services depends on the publisher’s consent management platform and the consent signals it passes to us. The publisher is responsible for its own consent implementation; the Services are designed to respond to consent signals, including IAB TCF strings, by enabling or limiting consent-dependent features.

## 8\. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to: access your personal data; have it corrected or completed; have it deleted; restrict or object to processing (including direct marketing, which we will always stop on request); receive your data in a portable format; withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent; and complain to a supervisory authority. Residents of certain other jurisdictions (for example, California under the CCPA/CPRA) may have equivalent rights, including rights to know, delete and correct, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

To exercise any right, contact [privacy@powerbeans.ai](mailto:privacy@powerbeans.ai). We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within the timescales required by applicable law. There is no fee unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive. If your request concerns data we process on behalf of a publisher (for example, listening activity on a publisher’s website), we will forward your request to that publisher or direct you to them, as the law requires us to act on the controller’s instructions.

## 9\. Security

We maintain technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data, including encryption in transit, access controls based on least privilege, credential and key management, logging and monitoring, backups, and incident response procedures. No internet service can be guaranteed to be completely secure; if we discover a personal data breach, we will notify affected parties and authorities where required by applicable law.

## 10\. Children

The Website, Platform and Services are business tools and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16 (or the higher age applicable in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact [privacy@powerbeans.ai](mailto:privacy@powerbeans.ai) and we will delete it.

## 11\. Third-party links and publisher properties

The Website may link to third-party websites and services we do not control; their privacy practices are their own. Likewise, publishers may deploy their own tags, pixels, cookies or advertising technologies alongside the Services on their properties; those are governed by the publisher’s privacy policy, not this one.

## 12\. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect legal requirements and how we operate. We will post the updated version on this page with a revised “Last updated” date and, for material changes, will provide additional notice through the Platform or by email where appropriate.
