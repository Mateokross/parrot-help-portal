This Data Processing Agreement (the “DPA”) is entered into between Powerbeans LTD, a company registered in England and Wales under company number 15423977, with registered office at 5 Brayford Square, London, United Kingdom, E1 0SG (“Powerbeans”), and the customer that accepts the Powerbeans [Terms and Conditions](https://powerbeans.ai/terms-and-conditions) (the “Terms”). This DPA forms part of, and is incorporated into, the Powerbeans Terms and Conditions (the “Terms”) and is accepted by the Publisher at account registration together with the Terms. It applies where Powerbeans processes Personal Data on behalf of the Publisher in the course of providing the Services.

## 1\. Definitions

1.1 In this DPA: “Data Protection Laws” means, as applicable, the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 (“EU GDPR”), the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR), the EU ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC as implemented, and any other applicable data protection or privacy laws.

1.2 “Personal Data”, “Controller”, “Processor”, “Data Subject”, “Processing”, “Personal Data Breach” and “Supervisory Authority” have the meanings given in Data Protection Laws.

1.3 “Publisher Personal Data” means Personal Data processed by Powerbeans on behalf of the Publisher in connection with the Services, as described in Annex I. “End User”, “Publisher Content”, “Services” and other capitalised terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Terms.

## 2\. Roles and scope

2.1 For Publisher Personal Data, the Publisher is the Controller (or, where the Publisher acts on behalf of another controller, a processor with authority to appoint Powerbeans as subprocessor) and Powerbeans is the Processor.

2.2 Powerbeans acts as an independent Controller — and this DPA does not apply — where it processes Personal Data for its own purposes, including account administration, billing and payment, fraud prevention, security, marketing, product improvement using aggregated or de-identified data, website operation and legal compliance. Such processing is described in the Powerbeans [Privacy Policy](https://powerbeans.ai/privacy-policy).

2.3 The subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the Processing, the types of Personal Data and the categories of Data Subjects are set out in Annex I.

2.4 By design, Powerbeans does not collect or store directly identifying information about End Users (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses) through the Services. Publisher Personal Data relating to End Users is limited to pseudonymous online identifiers and technical data — including cookie and consent-dependent identifiers, device and browser information, playback and engagement events, and general location derived from IP address — which constitute Personal Data under Data Protection Laws. The parties will interpret this DPA in light of this limited, pseudonymous scope of Processing.

## 3\. Publisher obligations

3.1 The Publisher is responsible for the lawfulness of the Processing, including establishing a lawful basis, providing required notices to End Users, and obtaining and maintaining any required consents (including cookie and ePrivacy consents through a compliant consent management platform) before Publisher Personal Data is processed through the Services.

3.2 The Publisher’s instructions to Powerbeans are set out in this DPA, the Terms and the configuration options selected by the Publisher in the Platform. The Publisher warrants that its instructions comply with Data Protection Laws.

3.3 The Publisher must not submit special category data, criminal offence data, children’s data or other highly sensitive Personal Data to the Services unless expressly agreed in writing by Powerbeans.

## 4\. Powerbeans obligations

4.1 Powerbeans will process Publisher Personal Data only on the Publisher’s documented instructions (as described in clause 3.2), including with regard to international transfers, unless required to do otherwise by law to which Powerbeans is subject; in that case Powerbeans will inform the Publisher of the legal requirement before Processing, unless the law prohibits this. Powerbeans will inform the Publisher if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws.

4.2 Powerbeans will ensure that persons authorised to process Publisher Personal Data are subject to appropriate obligations of confidentiality.

4.3 Powerbeans will implement and maintain the technical and organisational measures set out in Annex II, designed to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk.

4.4 Taking into account the nature of the Processing, Powerbeans will assist the Publisher by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as possible, in fulfilling the Publisher’s obligations to respond to Data Subject requests, and will assist the Publisher in ensuring compliance with its obligations regarding security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation, taking into account the information available to Powerbeans. Powerbeans may charge a reasonable fee for assistance that is materially disproportionate to the scale of the Publisher’s use of the Services.

4.5 If Powerbeans receives a request from a Data Subject relating to Publisher Personal Data, it will forward the request to the Publisher without undue delay and will not respond to it directly, except to direct the Data Subject to the Publisher or as instructed by the Publisher or required by law.

## 5\. Subprocessors

5.1 The Publisher provides general written authorisation for Powerbeans to engage the subprocessors listed in Annex III, and for those subprocessors to engage their own subprocessors, for the Processing of Publisher Personal Data.

5.2 Powerbeans will inform the Publisher of any intended addition or replacement of a subprocessor by notice by updating this DPA (30) days before the change takes effect. The Publisher may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within that period; if the parties cannot resolve the objection, the Publisher may cancel the affected Services with effect from the date the change takes effect, as its sole remedy.

5.3 Powerbeans will impose on each subprocessor, by written contract, data protection obligations materially no less protective than those in this DPA, and remains liable to the Publisher for the performance of its subprocessors’ obligations.

## 6\. International transfers

6.1 Publisher Personal Data may be processed in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area and other countries in which Powerbeans’ subprocessors operate.

6.2 Where Powerbeans transfers Publisher Personal Data outside the UK or the EEA to a country not covered by an adequacy decision, it will ensure an appropriate safeguard is in place under Data Protection Laws, including (as applicable) UK adequacy regulations and the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, EU adequacy decisions including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, and/or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, together with any supplementary measures reasonably required.

## 7\. Personal Data Breach

7.1 Powerbeans will notify the Publisher without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Publisher Personal Data. The notification will describe, to the extent known, the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address the breach and mitigate its effects. Powerbeans may provide information in phases as it becomes available.

7.2 Powerbeans’ notification of, or response to, a Personal Data Breach is not an acknowledgement of fault or liability. The Publisher is responsible for any notifications to Supervisory Authorities or Data Subjects required of it as Controller.

## 8\. Audit

8.1 Powerbeans will make available to the Publisher information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA. Audit requests must be sent in writing to [privacy@powerbeans.ai](mailto:privacy@powerbeans.ai). Powerbeans will respond to reasonable written questionnaires and provide copies of relevant existing documentation, policies or third-party reports, no more than once in any twelve (12) month period unless a Supervisory Authority requires otherwise or a Personal Data Breach has occurred.

8.2 An on-site or remote inspection is available only where required by Data Protection Laws or a Supervisory Authority, or where the Publisher has reasonable, documented grounds to believe Powerbeans is in material breach of this DPA; any such inspection requires at least thirty (30) days’ notice, is conducted during business hours at the Publisher’s cost, must not unreasonably disrupt Powerbeans’ operations, and is subject to Powerbeans’ confidentiality and security requirements.

## 9\. Return and deletion

9.1 Upon termination or expiry of the Agreement, Powerbeans will delete or anonymise Publisher Personal Data as follows, unless retention is required by law or necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims: Personal Data contained in or linked to Publisher Content and AI Outputs within ninety (90) days; and Personal Data contained in technical logs within six (6) months.

9.2 Given the pseudonymous nature of Publisher Personal Data (clause 2.4), the Services do not provide an export of End User Personal Data; the Publisher acknowledges that deletion or anonymisation under clause 9.1 satisfies Powerbeans’ return-or-deletion obligation. Powerbeans will confirm deletion in writing on request.

9.3 Powerbeans may retain and use data that has been aggregated or de-identified such that it no longer constitutes Personal Data (for example, aggregate playback and engagement statistics) for product improvement, benchmarking and analytics, during and after the term.

## 10\. Liability

10.1 This DPA is subject to the exclusions and limitations of liability in the Terms. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, each party’s total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this DPA will not exceed the Fees paid or payable by the Publisher to Powerbeans in the six (6) months preceding the event giving rise to liability. Nothing in this clause limits liability that cannot be limited under applicable law.

## 11\. General

11.1 This DPA takes effect on acceptance of the Terms and continues for as long as Powerbeans processes Publisher Personal Data.

11.2 In the event of conflict between this DPA and the Terms in relation to the Processing of Personal Data, this DPA prevails. Powerbeans may update this DPA in accordance with the change mechanism in the Terms, including where required by changes to Data Protection Laws.

11.3 Privacy contact: [privacy@powerbeans.ai](mailto:privacy@powerbeans.ai). This DPA is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction, as set out in the Terms.

## Annex II — Technical and Organisational Security Measures

Powerbeans implements and maintains, and requires its hosting providers to maintain, measures including the following, designed to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk and to the pseudonymous nature of the data processed:

1\. Encryption of Personal Data in transit using industry-standard protocols (TLS), and encryption at rest where supported by the relevant hosting and storage providers.

2\. Access controls based on the principle of least privilege: access to production systems and Personal Data is limited to authorised personnel who need it to perform their role, protected by individual credentials and multi-factor authentication where available.

3\. Credential and key management, including secure storage of API keys and secrets, and rotation or revocation of credentials where compromise is suspected.

4\. Logging and monitoring of production systems to detect and investigate unauthorised access, abuse and operational incidents.

5\. Regular backups of production data and configurations, with the ability to restore Services in the event of loss or corruption.

6\. Secure development practices, including separation of development and production environments and review of changes before deployment.

7\. Vendor management: selection of reputable infrastructure and service providers with documented security programmes, and contractual data protection obligations flowed down to subprocessors (clause 5.3).

8\. Incident response procedures for the identification, escalation, containment and remediation of security incidents, supporting the breach notification commitment in clause 7.

9\. Confidentiality obligations for all personnel with access to Personal Data (clause 4.2), and data minimisation by design, including the decision not to collect directly identifying End User data through the Services.
