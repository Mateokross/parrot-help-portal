## The difference attention makes

Standard display gets ignored. Audio placements inside active articles do not. The numbers are not close.

### Brand Recall

81%correct brand recall

They pressed play. They were paying attention. Audio creates memory anchors that text and banner ads don't.

### Click-Through Rate

\>1%CTR on audio + companion

Standard display averages below 0.1%. Ten times the performance because the user chose to be there.

### Ads shown in moments of active intent

Whether someone plays an audio article, browses a playlist, or opens a summary, they chose to engage. Your placement appears at that moment, not a passive slot between scrolls.

### Premium publishers, not long-tail inventory

Your placements run across established news brands: Forbes, Clarín, EFE, and others. Not content farms. Not aggregators.
