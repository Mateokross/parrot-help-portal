Powerbeans offers four plans for publishers of all sizes, from independent news sites to global media groups. All plans are billed monthly in advance and can be managed directly from your dashboard. Each tier unlocks more products, voices, and sites as you grow.

<Note>
  Plans and pricing are maintained in one place: **[powerbeans.ai/pricing](https://powerbeans.ai/pricing)**. Treat it as the source of truth for current prices, quotas, and feature availability — the summary below is for orientation only and may lag behind the live page.
</Note>

## Summaries Quota

<Note>
  **Full-length article audio counts as 5 summaries** against your monthly allowance. A standard AI text summary counts as 1. Unused summaries do not roll over to the next billing cycle — your quota resets each month. Audio generation pauses automatically if you reach your limit before the cycle ends.
</Note>

## Billing Details

All plans are billed monthly **in advance** by credit or debit card, processed securely via Stripe. Subscriptions auto-renew at the start of each billing cycle until you cancel.

- **Cancellation** takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You retain full access until that date.
- **Fees are non-refundable** except in cases of verified billing errors or duplicate charges. Contact [support@powerbeans.ai](mailto:support@powerbeans.ai) for billing disputes.
- **Upgrades** take effect immediately, with a prorated charge for the remainder of the current billing period.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the start of your next billing period.

## Free via Ads

Scale and Enterprise publishers may qualify to have their monthly subscription fee covered entirely by advertising revenue generated through the Powerbeans network. When your ad revenue meets or exceeds your plan fee, you effectively pay nothing — and standard revenue share applies to any amount above the platform cost.

This programme requires approval. To find out if your publication is eligible, contact [sales@powerbeans.ai](mailto:sales@powerbeans.ai).
