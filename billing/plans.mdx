## Free via Ads

Scale and Enterprise publishers may qualify to have their monthly subscription fee covered entirely by advertising revenue generated through the Powerbeans network. See [Free via Ads](/monetization/free-via-ads) for how it works and how to check eligibility.

## Billing Details

All plans are billed **in advance** by credit or debit card, processed securely via Stripe — monthly or annually, depending on the cycle you chose. Subscriptions auto-renew at the start of each billing cycle until you cancel.

- **Cancellation** takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You retain full access until that date.
- **Fees are non-refundable** except in cases of verified billing errors or duplicate charges. Contact [support@powerbeans.ai](mailto:support@powerbeans.ai) for billing disputes.
- **Upgrades** take effect immediately, with a prorated charge for the remainder of the current billing period. Upgrading keeps the billing cycle you're on — an annual Pro plan upgraded to Scale stays annual.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the start of your next billing period.

## Switching to annual

You can move from monthly to annual at any time from **Subscription** in your dashboard. When you do:

- You're charged the annual price today, less the unused part of the month you've already paid for.
- Your renewal date moves to one year from today.
- Any recurring add-ons move to their annual prices in the same change, and are charged at the same time.

You'll see the exact amount due before you confirm.

### Switching back to monthly

Moving from annual back to monthly isn't self-serve. Annual is a twelve-month commitment, so the change takes effect at your renewal date rather than immediately. Contact your account manager, or email [support@powerbeans.ai](mailto:support@powerbeans.ai), and the team will schedule it for you.
