Add-ons let you extend your plan's capacity without upgrading to the next tier.

## Available Add-Ons

| Add-On | How it's billed |
|---|---|
| Extra summaries (per 1,000) | Recurring, on your plan's billing cycle |
| Extra playlist | Recurring, on your plan's billing cycle |
| Extra site | Recurring, on your plan's billing cycle |
| Voice cloning (per voice) | One-time charge |

Current prices are on the [pricing page](https://powerbeans.ai/pricing) and on the **Subscription** page in your dashboard.

<Warning>
  On an annual plan, a recurring add-on is charged for a **full year on the day you buy it**, however far through your annual term you are. If you add a block of summaries two months before your renewal date, you pay twelve months then, and the add-on renews with your plan at the next renewal. If you're close to renewal and only need capacity for the rest of the term, contact your account manager before buying.
</Warning>

## Extra Summaries

If you use up your monthly summaries quota before it resets, you can add additional summary capacity in blocks of 1,000. This is a recurring add-on fixed to your subscription — once purchased, it renews automatically alongside your plan until you remove it, rather than being a one-time top-up. Each block adds 1,000 summaries to every month of your term.

<Tip>
  Full-length article audio counts as **5 summaries** per article, while a standard AI text summary counts as 1. A publication that generates mostly full-length audio uses its quota about five times faster.
</Tip>

## Extra Playlists

Each plan includes a set number of playlists — Pro includes 1, and Scale includes 3. If you need additional playlists beyond your plan's allowance, you can add more as a recurring add-on.

Extra playlists give you the flexibility to create topic-based or audience-specific playlists without moving up a plan tier. For example, a Pro publisher could run one general news playlist plus an extra sports playlist, rather than upgrading to Scale.

## Voice Cloning Add-On

Voice cloning — which replicates a writer's or presenter's real voice for AI narration, driving 2×–3× more clicks — is included in the **Scale** and **Enterprise** plans. If you're on Scale or Enterprise and need cloned voices beyond what your plan includes, you can add individual voices. This is a one-time charge, not a recurring add-on.

<Note>
  Voice cloning is not available on Starter or Pro plans, even as an add-on. To access voice cloning, upgrade to Scale or Enterprise. See [Plans](/billing/plans) for the full feature comparison.
</Note>

## Extra Sites

Your plan includes a set number of sites — the distinct domains or websites where Powerbeans is deployed. If you manage more sites than your plan allows, you can add additional sites as a recurring add-on.
