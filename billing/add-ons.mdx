Add-ons let you extend your plan's capacity without upgrading to the next tier.

## Available Add-Ons

| Add-On | Price |
|---|---|
| Extra summaries | $150/month per 1,000 summaries |
| Extra playlist | $100/month |
| Voice cloning (per voice) | $100/voice |
| Extra site | $150/month |

## Extra Summaries

If you use up your monthly summaries quota before the billing cycle resets, you can add additional summary capacity in blocks of 1,000 for **$150/month per block**. This is a recurring add-on fixed to your subscription — once purchased, it renews automatically every month alongside your plan until you remove it, rather than being a one-time top-up. Extra summaries are available on all plans and take effect immediately after purchase.

<Tip>
  Remember that full-length article audio counts as **5 summaries** per article, while a standard AI text summary counts as 1. If your publication primarily generates full-length audio, your quota will be consumed faster — plan accordingly or consider upgrading to a higher tier.
</Tip>

## Extra Playlists

Each plan includes a set number of playlists — Pro includes 1, and Scale includes 3. If you need additional playlists beyond your plan's allowance, you can add more for **$100/month per playlist**.

Extra playlists give you the flexibility to create topic-based or audience-specific playlists without moving up a plan tier. For example, a Pro publisher could run one general news playlist plus an extra sports playlist for $100/month, rather than upgrading to Scale.

## Voice Cloning Add-On

Voice cloning — which replicates a writer's or presenter's real voice for AI narration, driving 2×–3× more clicks — is included in the **Scale** and **Enterprise** plans. If you're on Scale or Enterprise and need cloned voices beyond what your plan includes, you can add individual voices for **$100/voice**.

<Note>
  Voice cloning is not available on Starter or Pro plans, even as an add-on. To access voice cloning, upgrade to Scale or Enterprise. See [Plans](/billing/plans) for the full feature comparison.
</Note>

## Extra Sites

Your plan includes a set number of sites — the distinct domains or websites where Powerbeans is deployed. If you manage more sites than your plan allows, you can add additional sites for **$150/month per site**.
