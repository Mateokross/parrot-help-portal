Powerbeans generates AI-narrated audio, audio summaries, and cloned voices in **more than 30 languages and accents**. Voices are ultra-realistic and human-sounding across nearly every supported language, so audio delivered to your readers carries the same tone and authenticity as the original article — regardless of the publication's primary language.

<Note>
  This page covers the languages available for **generated audio content**. It's separate from the language of the Powerbeans dashboard itself, which is currently English only.
</Note>

## How language is set

The language of generated audio follows the language of the source article — Powerbeans detects it automatically during content ingestion, so no manual configuration is required per article.

## Related customization

- **Voices** — choose from the AI voices available for your content's language. See the [Customization guide](/implementation/customization).
- **Voice cloning** — clone an author's voice in their own language. See [Voice Cloning](/implementation/voice-cloning).
- **Pronunciation dictionary** — add custom pronunciations for names, brands, or foreign words that the AI voice mispronounces, regardless of language. See the [Customization guide](/implementation/customization).

If you need audio in a language you don't see reflected in your account, contact [sales@powerbeans.ai](mailto:sales@powerbeans.ai) to confirm current coverage.
