Everything you configure, monitor, and pay for lives at [dashboard.powerbeans.ai](https://dashboard.powerbeans.ai).

## The Sidebar

| Area | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your next step, plus shortcuts into the products, the voices, your plan, and your team. The starting point when you're not sure what's outstanding. |
| **Products → Player** | Set up and customize the [Audio Player](/products/audio-player): content type, voices, colors, Gated Audio, and the setup checklist for getting it live. |
| **Products → Playlist** | Create and name your [Playlists](/products/playlist), choose how they're filled, and customize how the widget looks. |
| **Products → Text Summaries** | Set up and customize the [Text Summaries](/products/text-summaries) widget. |
| **Voice Library** | Listen to the available voices and choose which ones narrate your articles. |
| **Pronunciation** | See how [pronunciation](/advanced/pronunciation) is handled and request entries for words that come out wrong. |
| **Monetization** | Review the ad formats each product runs and set monetization up for your sites. More in the [Monetization overview](/monetization/overview). |
| **Performance** | Engagement, audio, and revenue reporting for your sites, with a Glossary tab defining every metric. |
| **Subscription** | Your plan, your monthly summaries, add-ons, and billing. See [Plans](/billing/plans) and [Add-Ons](/billing/add-ons). |
| **Settings → Team** | Invite colleagues, resend or revoke invitations, and remove people. |
| **Settings → Account** | Your profile, your company details, and your password. |
| **Settings → Sites** | Add and manage the sites where Powerbeans runs. A site must exist here before anything else works. |
| **Settings → API** | Generate and copy your API key, and open the API reference. |
