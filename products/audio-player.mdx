The results speak for themselves: **85% of audio summaries that are started are completed**, making it one of the most engaging formats you can add to your site.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Embed the Widget">
    Paste the Audio Player snippet into your CMS template. The widget loads asynchronously and appears on every article page automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your Content Is Ingested">
    Powerbeans sets up a content ingestion on your site, or you push articles directly via the API. No manual uploads needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Audio Is Generated Automatically">
    High-quality AI audio is produced for each article and published automatically. New articles are processed as they are published.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Carousel

The Carousel feature enables continuous audio playback across articles — when one piece finishes, the next queued article starts playing automatically. This lets your listeners consume content hands-free while commuting, exercising, or doing chores, without ever returning to their screen.

Carousel drives deeper sessions: publishers using it see an average session duration of **2.5 minutes** — well above typical audio engagement benchmarks.

<Note>
  Carousel is available from the **Pro plan** onwards. [See plans](/billing/plans) to find out which tier is right for you.

## Voice Cloning

Voice Cloning replicates each writer's unique voice, so articles are narrated by the actual author rather than a generic AI voice. This adds authenticity and a personal connection that generic text-to-speech cannot replicate. Publishers who enable Voice Cloning see **2–3× increased usage** for articles read by the author's own voice.

<Note>
  Voice Cloning is available from the **Scale plan** onwards. [See plans](/billing/plans) to find out which tier is right for you.
</Note>

## Customization

You can tailor the Audio Player to match your site's identity and editorial needs. Available on every plan:

- **Content type** — Summary, Full content, or Both (Both requires the Scale plan and above)
- **Voices** — choose a voice from the available AI voice library, or enable Voice Cloning
- **Colors** — theme color and play/pause icon color
- **New-visitor hint** — a one-time nudge letting first-time visitors know they can listen, on by default
- **Minimum date for audio generation** — skip automatic audio generation for articles published before a chosen date
- **Advertising** — configure ad placements and formats

## Standards & Compatibility

- **No page slowdown** — The widget loads asynchronously from a CDN and is cached in the browser for 30 days, so it never delays your page render.
- **Accessible by default** — Passes WCAG 2.2, with labeled controls, ARIA labels, visible focus states, and screen-reader-friendly playback text throughout.
- **GDPR-aligned** — Session and interaction data drive playback and analytics. Consent determines what is stored and for how long.
- **IAB TCF ready** — Works with IAB TCF consent signals from your CMP. Consent status controls what gets stored and how ads are served.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do you recommend summary audios over full-content audios?">
    Summary audios have a **2.5x higher completion rate** than full-content audios, so listeners are far more likely to finish what they start. Pages with summary audios also see higher click rates and usage rates overall — shorter, focused narration fits how people consume content on the go better than a full read-through does.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I only want audio on some articles, not my whole site?">
    Only add the widget script to the pages you want processed — nothing else is needed. Powerbeans only generates audio for pages where the script is present, so you control the rollout by controlling where the snippet lives in your CMS template.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when multiple voices are selected for one site or player?">
    One of the selected voices is picked at random for each article.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
