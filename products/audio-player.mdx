The Powerbeans Audio Player transforms every article on your site into high-quality, AI-narrated audio — fully automatically, with no editorial effort required. Once the widget is embedded, Powerbeans ingests your content and generates audio for each piece, giving your audience a hands-free way to consume your journalism. The results speak for themselves: **85% of audios that are started are completed**, making it one of the most engaging formats you can add to your site.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Embed the Widget">
    Paste the Audio Player snippet into your CMS template. The widget loads asynchronously and appears on every article page automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Content Is Ingested">
    Powerbeans sets up a content ingestion integration with your CMS, or you push articles directly via the API. No manual uploads needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Audio Is Generated Automatically">
    High-quality AI audio is produced for each article and published without any editorial intervention. New articles are processed as they are published.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Carousel

The Carousel feature enables continuous audio playback across articles — when one piece finishes, the next queued article starts playing automatically. This lets your listeners consume content hands-free while commuting, exercising, or doing chores, without ever returning to their screen.

Carousel drives deeper sessions: publishers using it see an average session duration of **2.5 minutes** — well above typical audio engagement benchmarks.

## Voice Cloning

Voice Cloning replicates each writer's unique voice, so articles are narrated by the actual author rather than a generic AI voice. This adds authenticity and a personal connection that generic text-to-speech cannot replicate. Publishers who enable Voice Cloning see **2–3× increased usage** for articles read by the author's own voice.

<Note>
  Voice Cloning is available from the **Scale plan** onwards. [See plans](/billing/plans) to find out which tier is right for you.
</Note>

## Customization

You can tailor the Audio Player to match your site's identity and editorial needs. Adjustable settings include:

- **Content type** — summaries, full-length audio, or both
- **Voices** — choose from available AI voices or enable Voice Cloning
- **Advertising** — configure ad placements and formats
- **Colors** — match your brand's visual style
- **Texts** — localize labels, CTAs, and UI copy

For a full breakdown of available options, see the [Customization guide](/implementation/customization).

## Standards & Compatibility

- **No page slowdown** — The widget loads asynchronously from a CDN and is cached in the browser for 30 days, so it never delays your page render.
- **Accessible by default** — Passes WCAG 2.2, with labeled controls, ARIA labels, visible focus states, and screen-reader-friendly playback text throughout.
- **GDPR-aligned** — Session and interaction data drive playback and analytics. Consent determines what is stored and for how long.
- **IAB TCF ready** — Works with IAB TCF consent signals from your CMP. Consent status controls what gets stored and how ads are served.
