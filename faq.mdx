Short answers to common questions. For billing-specific questions, see the [Billing FAQ](/billing/faq).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why should I add summaries alongside audio?">
    Text Summaries give readers a fast, AI-generated overview of an article before they commit to reading or listening to the full piece. They're not a replacement for your content — they act as a motivational gateway: **81% of readers find summaries useful**, and readers who interact with them explore **2× more pages per session** than those who don't. See [Text Summaries](/products/text-summaries) for the full picture.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I only want audio on some articles, not my whole site?">
    Only add the widget script to the pages you want processed — nothing else is needed. Powerbeans only generates audio for pages where the script is present, so you control the rollout by controlling where the snippet lives in your CMS template. See [Advanced Integration](/implementation/advanced-integration) for more.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when multiple voices are selected for one site or player?">
    This depends on your specific configuration and isn't fully documented yet — contact [support@powerbeans.ai](mailto:support@powerbeans.ai) or your account manager to confirm behavior for your setup before enabling multiple voices.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I hit my summaries or character limit?">
    Generation pauses automatically once your monthly quota is reached, and resumes when your billing cycle resets — or immediately if you purchase additional summaries. See [Add-Ons](/billing/add-ons) and the [Billing FAQ](/billing/faq) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
