The full, legally binding Privacy Policy is available at [powerbeans.ai/privacy-policy](https://powerbeans.ai/privacy-policy).

  </Card>
  <Card title="Site and integration data" icon="globe">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Billing and transactions" icon="credit-card">

  </Card>

## Data collected from your readers

When your Powerbeans widgets are live, Powerbeans processes the following data **on your behalf** as a data processor — you remain the data controller for this data:

- **Pseudonymous identifiers** — cookie and consent-dependent identifiers used for playback continuity and ad delivery
- **Device and browser information** — type, version, OS, used to deliver the right player experience
- **Playback and engagement events** — play, pause, completion, skip, and article interactions
- **General location from IP address** — country and region level; not stored as PII
- **No directly identifying data** — by design, Powerbeans does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses from your end users

For more detail on how this processing works and your obligations, see the [GDPR & Consent](/compliance/gdpr-consent) page.

## How Powerbeans uses your data

Where Powerbeans acts as controller — processing data about you and your account — it relies on the following legal bases:

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Providing the Platform | Contract |
| Billing and payments | Contract / legal obligation |
| Security and fraud prevention | Legitimate interests |
| Product analytics | Legitimate interests / consent |
| Marketing | Legitimate interests / consent |
| Legal compliance | Legal obligation |

Where Powerbeans relies on legitimate interests, those interests have been balanced against your rights. You can object to legitimate-interest processing at any time by contacting [privacy@powerbeans.ai](mailto:privacy@powerbeans.ai).

## Data retention

Powerbeans keeps personal data only as long as it is needed:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Account and billing records — 6 years">
    Account details, invoices, and transaction records are retained for six years for tax, accounting, and legal compliance purposes. This applies even after you cancel your subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Publisher content and AI outputs — 90 days post-termination">
    Your articles, audio files, summaries, and any personal data within them are deleted or anonymised within 90 days of your agreement ending.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Technical logs — up to 6 months">
    Server and application logs used for security monitoring and incident investigation are retained for up to six months.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Marketing data — until opt-out">
    Marketing communications data is retained until you opt out or Powerbeans no longer has a lawful basis to hold it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Your rights

As a publisher and account holder, you have the following rights over your personal data held by Powerbeans:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal data Powerbeans holds about you
- **Correction** — ask Powerbeans to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Deletion** — request erasure of your data where there is no lawful reason to retain it
- **Portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Objection** — object to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing
- **Restriction** — ask Powerbeans to restrict processing in certain circumstances
- **Withdraw consent** — where processing relies on consent, withdraw it at any time

To exercise any right, contact [privacy@powerbeans.ai](mailto:privacy@powerbeans.ai). Powerbeans may need to verify your identity and will respond within the timescales required by applicable law. There is no fee unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

<Tip>
  If your request concerns data processed on behalf of a publisher — for example, listening activity on your own site — that falls under the DPA. Powerbeans will direct you accordingly, as it is required to act on the controller's instructions in those cases.
</Tip>

## International transfers

Your data may be processed in the **UK, EEA, and US**, where Powerbeans and its subprocessors operate. Where transfers occur to countries without an adequacy decision, Powerbeans puts appropriate safeguards in place, including:

- UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or Addendum
- EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
- UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (where applicable)

Supplementary measures are applied where required by the nature of the transfer.
