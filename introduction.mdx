Powerbeans is an AI-powered engagement platform designed specifically for publishers and media companies. You embed a single widget into your CMS, and Powerbeans automatically transforms your article catalog into AI-narrated audio, bingeable playlists, and text summaries — complete with built-in monetization. Trusted by Forbes, Clarín, EFE, Milenio, TV Azteca, El Colombiano, La Voz del Interior, and dozens of other leading publications, Powerbeans delivers measurable audience engagement and new ad revenue without requiring any editorial effort or technical overhead.

## How It Works

Powerbeans plugs into your existing publishing workflow in three steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Embed the Widget">
    Paste one line of JavaScript into your CMS template. The widget loads asynchronously from a CDN so your page speed is unaffected. Each product — Audio Player, Playlist, Text Summaries — has its own widget snippet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Content Is Ingested">
    Powerbeans sets up a content ingestion integration with your CMS, or you can send articles directly via API. New articles are picked up and queued for processing automatically — no manual uploads, no RSS wrangling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Audio and Summaries Go Live">
    Powerbeans generates high-quality AI audio and text summaries for each article and publishes them directly on your site. Ad slots are activated automatically through Google MCM, supporting both programmatic and direct sales.
  </Step>
</Steps>
