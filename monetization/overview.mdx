Powerbeans builds ad revenue into every product. When you go live, new ad surfaces are created automatically inside your audio and summary widgets — there is no separate ad stack to configure, no demand partners to negotiate with, and no operations overhead to absorb. You get the revenue; Powerbeans handles the rest.

## How Monetization Works

Powerbeans acts as your monetization partner end-to-end. As a Google MCM Partner, Powerbeans gives you access to Google AdX demand alongside 35+ top-tier SSPs — all connected and managed on your behalf. You do not need to set up your own ad server or manage individual demand relationships.

- **Programmatic and direct demand** — Powerbeans connects you to real-time programmatic auctions through Google AdX and 35+ SSPs, and also supports private marketplace deals.
- **End-to-end management** — ad configuration, demand optimization, and quality controls are managed for you. Your job is to publish content; Powerbeans handles the ad layer.
- **Bring your own demand (Enterprise)** — if you have existing direct-sold campaigns or a preferred ad server, Enterprise publishers can supply their own VAST tags to run alongside Powerbeans' programmatic fill.

## What's Required to Go Live with Monetization

Before ads can serve on your content, a few prerequisites must be in place:

- **Consent signals** — your CMP must pass valid IAB TCF consent signals. Without compliant consent, ads cannot serve to users in applicable jurisdictions. See [GDPR & Consent](/compliance/gdpr-consent) for implementation details.
- **ads.txt** — you must add Powerbeans' ads.txt entries to your domain. This authorizes Powerbeans to sell your inventory and is required by most programmatic buyers.
- **Eligibility review** — some plans and publishers require an approval step before monetization activates. Your account manager will walk you through this during onboarding.

<Note>
  Revenue is subject to demand availability, traffic quality, and consent signals. Dashboard metrics may be estimated pending final monthly reconciliation with demand partners.
</Note>

## Ad Quality and Brand Safety

Ad quality controls and brand safety filters are built into Powerbeans' monetization layer. You do not need to configure or manage these separately. Ads running through Powerbeans are screened against brand safety standards before serving, helping to protect your editorial reputation and reader experience.

## Reporting

Powerbeans gives you full visibility into your monetization performance. From the dashboard you can track earnings, fill rates, and CPMs broken down by product and time period. See [Reporting & Dashboard](/reporting/dashboard) for a full walkthrough of available metrics.

  Publishers on Scale and Enterprise plans may qualify for **Free-via-Ads**, where ad revenue covers your monthly subscription fee. Any surplus above your platform cost is then split under the standard revenue share. See [Revenue Share](/monetization/revenue-share) for details.
</Tip>
