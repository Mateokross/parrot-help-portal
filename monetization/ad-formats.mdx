Each Powerbeans product ships with built-in ad formats that activate as part of your monetization configuration. You do not need to set up separate ad tags, configure individual placements, or manage creative trafficking — formats go live automatically when monetization is enabled. The only exception is Enterprise publishers who choose to supply their own VAST tags to bring custom direct demand.

## Formats by Product

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Audio Player">
    The Audio Player runs audio and display formats directly within the listening experience. Because the reader actively chose to play audio, ad placement occurs at a moment of genuine attention.

    - **Pre-roll audio ads** — a short audio ad plays before the article audio begins. This is the highest-attention placement in the player, delivered to a listener who just pressed play.
    - **Post-roll audio ads** — an audio ad plays after the article audio finishes. Reaches listeners who stayed engaged through the full piece.
    - **Companion banners** — a display banner shown alongside audio ads, reinforcing the audio message with a visual. Companion banners are served in sync with pre-roll and post-roll audio placements.
    - **Display formats** — standard display placements within the player interface, serving independently of audio ad breaks.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Playlist">
    The Playlist widget surfaces longer listening sessions, making it suitable for sequential ad delivery across multiple articles.

    - **Audio pre-roll** — an audio ad plays before the playlist starts. Delivered to a listener at the moment they commit to a session of content.
    - **Video formats** — video ad placements run during playback, taking advantage of the video-capable player surface and the higher CPMs that video demand commands.
    - **Display formats (before playback)** — display placements shown on the playlist before the listener begins, capturing attention during the browsing moment.
    - **Display formats (during playback)** — display placements that continue to serve while the listener is actively engaged with the playlist.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Text Summaries">
    The Text Summaries widget embeds display advertising directly inside the summary experience, without requiring the reader to start audio.

    - **Embedded display placements** — display ads are placed within the summary widget itself, appearing as the reader engages with the AI-generated summary.
    - **Interaction-triggered display** — optionally, display placements can remain hidden until the reader interacts with the summary widget, ensuring ads only appear when active engagement is confirmed.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Why These Formats Perform

Standard display inventory placed between scrolled paragraphs captures little attention. Powerbeans formats are different because they appear inside surfaces the reader deliberately opened — a player they pressed play on, a playlist they browsed to, a summary they expanded.

- **81% correct brand recall** for audio ads served through Powerbeans placements. Listeners who chose to engage remember what they heard.
- **Over 1% CTR** on audio combined with companion banners. The industry average for standard display is below 0.1% — that is more than ten times the engagement.
- **Active intent at the moment of serving** — whether a reader starts an audio article, opens a playlist, or expands a summary, they made a deliberate choice to engage. Your ads appear at that moment, not in a passive scroll position.

<Note>
  Ad formats run automatically once monetization is enabled on your account. You do not need to configure individual ad tags or manage creative placements unless you are on the Enterprise plan and supplying your own VAST tags.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Enterprise publishers can supply their own VAST tags to inject direct-sold or preferred demand alongside Powerbeans' programmatic fill — so your existing ad sales relationships continue to work.
</Tip>
