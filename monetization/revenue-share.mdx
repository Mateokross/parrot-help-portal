Powerbeans shares advertising revenue with you every month. The split depends on your plan, and eligible publishers on Scale and Enterprise can have their monthly subscription fee covered entirely by ad earnings before any surplus is paid out. Here is how the numbers work.

## Revenue Share Rates

Your publisher share is calculated after monthly reconciliation with demand partners. The split by plan is:

| Plan | Publisher Share | Powerbeans Share |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 50% | 50% |
| Pro | 50% | 50% |
| Scale | 50% | 50% |
| Enterprise | 70% | 30% |

Enterprise publishers keep a larger share of every dollar earned, reflecting the scale of traffic and the additional flexibility — including preferred revenue share and the ability to bring your own ad tags — that the plan provides.

## Free-via-Ads

Free-via-Ads is an option available to eligible publishers on the Scale and Enterprise plans. Instead of paying your monthly platform fee out of pocket, your ad earnings cover it first.

**How it works:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ad revenue accrues">
    Ads run across your Powerbeans widgets as normal. Revenue accumulates throughout the month based on impressions and demand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Platform fee is offset">
    At the end of the month, your ad earnings are applied first against your monthly subscription fee. If ad revenue equals or exceeds your plan cost, your effective fee for that month is zero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Surplus is shared">
    Any ad earnings above the platform fee are then split between you and Powerbeans according to the revenue share rates in the table above — 50/50 for Scale, 70/30 for Enterprise.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Free-via-Ads requires sales-assisted approval. Contact [sales@powerbeans.ai](mailto:sales@powerbeans.ai) to find out whether your site qualifies.

<Tip>
  If your site generates significant traffic, the Free-via-Ads model can eliminate your monthly subscription cost entirely. High-traffic Scale and Enterprise publishers are the most likely to benefit.
</Tip>

## Payout Schedule

- **Payment timing** — payouts are made within 60 calendar days of the end of the month in which revenues are received from demand partners. For example, January ad revenue is paid out by the end of March.
- **Minimum payout threshold** — the minimum payout amount is USD 200. If your balance is below this at the end of a given month, it rolls over and accumulates until the threshold is reached.
- **Payment methods** — bank transfer or PayPal. Your preferred method can be configured in your account settings.
- **Fee offset before payout** — your publisher share first offsets any outstanding platform fees before a net surplus is paid to you. This applies whether or not you are on the Free-via-Ads option.

## Invalid Traffic and Adjustments

<Note>
  Powerbeans applies ad quality controls and brand safety filters across all inventory. Despite these safeguards, final amounts may be adjusted or withheld in cases of invalid traffic, fraudulent activity, or advertiser deductions applied during reconciliation. All amounts shown in the dashboard are estimates until monthly reconciliation with demand partners is complete and final figures are confirmed.
</Note>

## ads.txt Requirement

<Warning>
  You must implement Powerbeans' ads.txt entries on your domain before monetization can go live. Without a valid ads.txt file, most programmatic buyers will not bid on your inventory, significantly reducing fill and revenue. Contact your account manager for the exact entries to add.
</Warning>
