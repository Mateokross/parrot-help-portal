Powerbeans shares advertising revenue with you every month. The split depends on your plan. Here is how the numbers work.

## Revenue Share Rates

Your publisher share is calculated after monthly reconciliation with demand partners. The split by plan is:

| Plan | Publisher Share | Powerbeans Share |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 50% | 50% |
| Pro | 50% | 50% |
| Scale | 50% | 50% |
| Enterprise | 70% | 30% |

Eligible publishers on Scale and Enterprise can also have their monthly subscription fee covered entirely by ad earnings before any surplus is split — see [Free via Ads](/monetization/free-via-ads).

For when and how your share is actually paid out, see [Payouts](/monetization/payouts).
